# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sub2API administrators who manage provider accounts, groups, availability, routing, and operational policies from a dense admin console.

## Product Purpose

Provide a reliable operations console for configuring accounts and gateway behavior. The account management surface is the primary place for day-to-day account operations, while system settings should remain aligned with the upstream product's general configuration model. Custom features should be grouped in one discoverable area so they can grow without fragmenting the core settings experience.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, operational. The interface should feel trustworthy and efficient under repeated administrative use, with clear state, restrained emphasis, and predictable interactions.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style hero layouts, decorative card grids, oversized promotional copy, and visually noisy dashboard ornamentation. Do not make custom features look like a separate product or hide critical state behind unexplained controls.

## Design Principles

- Keep upstream-compatible settings recognizable and stable.
- Put high-frequency account operations close to the accounts they affect.
- Make custom behavior explicit, scoped, and easy to extend.
- Prefer dense, scannable layouts with clear state and reversible controls.
- Preserve existing component, spacing, and localization conventions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use the existing accessible controls and keyboard navigation patterns. Keep labels and status text available in both Chinese and English, maintain readable contrast, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
